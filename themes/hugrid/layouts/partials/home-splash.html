The archive

A life remembered
through the post

In 2019, forty-two years after Myrtle’s death, a box of her cherished postcards was found. Together, they preserve the landscapes, friendships, and everyday voice of an independent young woman in early twentieth-century California.

Myrtle Caroline Williams Fleener was born in Tulare County in 1883 and lived until 1977. She was an accomplished seamstress, a spirited grandmother, and the careful keeper of these small paper histories.

Dating from 1904–1926, the collection brings together period illustration, historic views, and intimate correspondence. Each card is presented front and back with its surviving postmark and message.
